## Quoridor Game
Quoridor is a strategy-based board game that challenges players to think ahead, adapt quickly, and outmaneuver their opponents. The game is played on a 9×9 grid board, where each player controls a pawn that starts on one side of the board. The main objective is simple: be the first player to move your pawn to the opposite side of the board.

### What makes the game unique is the wall-placement mechanic. On each turn, a player can either:

- Move their pawn one space in any direction (up, down, left, or right), or
- Place a wall segment on the board to block and redirect opponents.

Walls can be placed vertically or horizontally between spaces, creating obstacles and forcing players to take longer routes. However, walls cannot completely trap an opponent — every player must always have at least one possible path to reach their goal.

Each player has a limited number of walls, so deciding when to use them becomes a major strategic element. A player who uses walls too early may run out later, while a player who focuses only on movement may get blocked and lose their advantage.

### The game requires:

- Strategic planning
- Spatial awareness
- Defensive and offensive thinking
- Predicting opponents’ moves
- Efficient resource management

Although the rules are easy to learn, matches can become highly competitive because every move changes the possible paths on the board. This balance between simplicity and deep strategy is what makes Quoridor popular among both casual players and serious strategy game fans.
